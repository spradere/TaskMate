Open a file at a given line:
	geany +NUM_LINE file.c

Use ~/bin/go to select/paste an error from compiler output:
	go path/file.c:48

Open all files:
	geany $(find . -type f -name '*.h')
	find . -type f \( -name "*.c" -o -name "*.h" \) -print0 | xargs -0 geany &

Display a Make variable:
	make -V VAR_NAME

View preprocessor expansion only (-E):
clang -dM -E -I/root/code/TaskMate/TaskMate_current/src/ src/autoCode/options.c > xmacro
